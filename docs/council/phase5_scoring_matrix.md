## 1. Executive Scoring Overview

Phase 5 represents the definitive quantitative synthesis of the Learning Council's deliberation. Following the blind divergence of 256 innovations (Phase 1), thematic consolidation (Phase 2), cross-seat nominations (Phase 3), and adversarial debate with hardened hybrid syntheses (Phase 4), all seven council seats independently evaluated every surviving Top 10 Finalist across six weighted criteria.

### Key Methodological Highlights
1. **Independent Evaluation Across 7 Expert Seats**: Every seat evaluated each finalist strictly through its assigned professional mandate and persona, ensuring that learning psychology, instructional integrity, game design, student mental health, psychometrics, systems architecture, and frugal unit economics were fully represented.
2. **Weighted Multi-Criteria Protocol**: Every finalist was evaluated across 6 rigorous criteria totaling 55 points per seat and 385 points across the entire council:
   - **Learning impact** (Weight: ×3, max 15 pts)
   - **Evidence strength** (Weight: ×2, max 10 pts)
   - **Feasibility within constraints** (Weight: ×2, max 10 pts)
   - **Learner engagement** (Weight: ×2, max 10 pts)
   - **Cost-efficiency** (Weight: ×1, max 5 pts)
   - **Measurability** (Weight: ×1, max 5 pts)
3. **Debate-Informed Hardened Specifications**: For the Top 5 contested finalists (`CON-028`, `CON-001`, `CON-070`, `CON-031`, `CON-067`), seats scored the *hardened hybrid specifications* codified in the Phase 4 Debate Log rather than the naive initial proposals.
4. **Variance & Dissent Documentation**: Rather than forcing artificial uniformity, the scoring framework captured authentic inter-seat tensions (such as pedagogical impact vs infrastructure utility, or ludic tension vs cognitive fatigue), codifying explicit minority caveats, operational conditions, and binding guardrails.
