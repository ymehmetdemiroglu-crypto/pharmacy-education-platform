# Plan 03-01: EassonStedmanStage (3D Chiral Alignment Widget)

## Objective
Implement `EassonStedmanStage` in `packages/widgets`, a 3D interactive chiral alignment simulation widget demonstrating the Easson-Stedman 3-point binding hypothesis, eudismic ratio, and enantiomeric selective affinity using GPU-accelerated CSS 3D transforms, WCAG 2.2 accessible steppers, and transparent thermodynamic equations.

## Scientific & Biophysical Ground Truth
- Derived strictly from `materials/medchem/İlaçlarda  İzomeri.pdf` slides 11-20, 25, 28:
  - Slide 25: Epinephrine and adrenergic receptor stereoselectivity.
  - Slide 28: Eutomer, distomer, eudismic ratio, and Pfeiffer's rule.
- Pharmacophore Model (3 distinct binding interaction loci):
  1. **Locus A**: Aromatic binding pocket ($\pi-\pi$ stacking, $\Delta G_A \approx -4.5 \text{ kcal/mol}$).
  2. **Locus B**: Hydrogen bond donor/acceptor cleft (hydroxyl OH, $\Delta G_B \approx -3.0 \text{ kcal/mol}$).
  3. **Locus C**: Anionic salt bridge carboxylate ($\text{NH}_2^+ \dots ^-\text{OOC-Asp}$, $\Delta G_C \approx -4.0 \text{ kcal/mol}$).
- Enantiomeric Behavior:
  - **Eutomer ((S)-propranolol / (R)-epinephrine)**: Rotational alignment enables simultaneous binding to Loci A, B, and C:
    $$\Delta G_{\text{eutomer}} = \Delta G_A + \Delta G_B + \Delta G_C \approx -11.5 \text{ kcal/mol}$$
  - **Distomer ((R)-propranolol / (S)-epinephrine)**: When Loci A and C dock, the hydroxyl group projects outward into bulk solvent or clashes sterically:
    $$\Delta G_{\text{distomer}} = \Delta G_A + \Delta G_C \approx -8.5 \text{ kcal/mol}$$
  - **Eudismic Affinity Ratio**:
    $$\text{Eudismic Ratio} = \frac{K_{d,\text{distomer}}}{K_{d,\text{eutomer}}} = e^{\frac{-\Delta\Delta G}{RT}} \approx e^{\frac{3.0}{0.592}} \approx 150\times$$

## UX & Accessibility Requirements
- **3D Visualization**: GPU-composited CSS 3D transforms (`transform: perspective(700px) rotateX(...) rotateY(...) rotateZ(...) translate3d(...)`) with pure `transform` and `opacity` transitions (150–250ms). Zero layout thrashing (`width`, `height`, `margin` never animated).
- **Interactive Controls**:
  - Touch/mouse drag rotation in 3D.
  - Discrete keyboard stepper buttons (`rotateLeft`, `rotateRight`, `flipEnantiomer`, `snapToPocket`) with strict ARIA labels, `Tab`, `Enter`, and `Space` activation (WCAG 2.2 compliant).
- **Live Biophysical Readout**:
  - Displays docked contacts ($3/3$ for eutomer $\implies \text{Active}$; $2/3$ for distomer $\implies \text{Submaximal/Inactive}$).
  - Live calculated free energy $\Delta G$ and relative affinity $K_d$.
- **Model Illustration Notice**:
  - Permanent banner displaying the Easson-Stedman thermodynamic model disclaimer and citations.
