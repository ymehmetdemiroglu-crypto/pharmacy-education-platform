# Plan 03-02: ReceptorOperationalModel (Black-Leff Operational Model Widget)

## Objective
Implement `ReceptorOperationalModel` in `packages/widgets`, an interactive pharmacodynamics simulation widget that implements the closed-form Black & Leff (1983) operational model of agonism, replacing simplistic Hill models and demonstrating spare receptors, operational efficacy ($\tau$), and transducer slope ($n$).

## Scientific & Biophysical Ground Truth
- Derived from:
  - `materials/pharmacology/İlaç Reseptör Etkileşimi-Kimyasal Bağlar.pdf` slides 4, 6 (Receptor binding and response coupling).
  - Black & Leff (Proc R Soc Lond B 1983; 220:141-162) and Goodman & Gilman 14th ed. Chapter 3.
- Closed-Form Governing Equations:
  $$\text{Effect} = \frac{E_{\max} \cdot \tau^n \cdot [A]^n}{(K_A + [A])^n + \tau^n \cdot [A]^n}$$
  where:
  - $[A]$: Agonist concentration ($M$).
  - $K_A$: Agonist-receptor equilibrium dissociation constant ($M$).
  - $\tau = \frac{[R_t]}{K_E}$: Operational efficacy (transducer ratio of total receptor pool $[R_t]$ to sensitivity parameter $K_E$).
  - $n$: Transducer slope / cooperativity parameter.
- Key Pharmacological Derivations:
  - Observed maximum response: $E_{\max,\text{obs}} = E_{\max} \cdot \frac{\tau^n}{1 + \tau^n}$.
  - Operational potency: $EC_{50} = \frac{K_A}{(2 + \tau^n)^{1/n} - 1} \xrightarrow{n=1} \frac{K_A}{1 + \tau}$.
  - **Spare Receptor Demonstration**: When $\tau \gg 1$ (e.g. $\tau = 10$), $EC_{50} = \frac{K_A}{11}$. At $[A] = EC_{50}$, receptor fractional occupancy is only:
    $$\rho = \frac{[A]}{K_A + [A]} = \frac{K_A / 11}{K_A + K_A / 11} = \frac{1}{12} \approx 8.3\%$$
    Thus, a 50% maximal tissue response occurs with less than 9% receptor occupancy $\implies$ direct, rigorous demonstration of spare receptors without hand-waving!
  - **Partial Agonist Demonstration**: When $\tau \sim 0.5$, $E_{\max,\text{obs}} = \frac{0.5}{1.5} E_{\max} \approx 33.3\%$, regardless of how high $[A]$ is driven.

## UX & Accessibility Requirements
- **Live Vector SVG Curve Plotting**:
  - Closed-form logarithmic concentration grid ($10^{-10}$ to $10^{-3} \text{ M}$).
  - Simultaneous dual-curve comparison: Agonist ($A$, full), Partial Agonist ($B$), and Spare Receptor depleted system (following irreversible antagonist wash, reducing $[R_t]$ and dropping $\tau$).
  - Direct display of calculated $EC_{50}$, $E_{\max,\text{obs}}$, and receptor occupancy $\rho_{50}$.
- **Neo-Brutalist Visual Design**:
  - 4px high-contrast borders `#000000`, 6px hard drop shadows.
  - Interactive parameter sliders: $\tau$ (0.1 to 20), $K_A$ ($10^{-9}$ to $10^{-4}$ M), transducer slope $n$ (0.5 to 2.5).
  - WCAG 2.2 discrete `+` / `-` keyboard stepper buttons with ARIA labels and value announcements.
- **Model Illustration Notice**:
  - Exposes governing Black-Leff equations and literature citations.
