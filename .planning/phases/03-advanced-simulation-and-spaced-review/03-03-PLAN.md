# Plan 03-03: PkCockpit (Multi-Dose Pharmacokinetic Simulator)

## Objective
Implement `PkCockpit` in `packages/widgets`, an interactive, multi-dose pharmacokinetic cockpit simulator with analytical superposition math, steady-state accumulation metrics, and live visual therapeutic window monitoring with peak/trough safety alerts.

## Scientific & Biophysical Ground Truth
- Derived from:
  - `materials/pharmacology/İlaç metabolizması-2026.pdf` slides 2, 3 (elimination, clearance, and first-pass).
  - Rowland & Tozer's Clinical Pharmacokinetics and Goodman & Gilman 14th ed. Chapter 2.
- Closed-Form Governing Equations:
  - Single-dose oral concentration:
    $$C(t) = \frac{F \cdot \text{Dose} \cdot k_a}{V_d \cdot (k_a - k_e)} \left( e^{-k_e t} - e^{-k_a t} \right)$$
  - Multi-dose oral accumulation (dose $n$ at interval $\tau$):
    $$C_n(t') = \frac{F \cdot \text{Dose} \cdot k_a}{V_d \cdot (k_a - k_e)} \left[ \left(\frac{1 - e^{-n k_e \tau}}{1 - e^{-k_e \tau}}\right) e^{-k_e t'} - \left(\frac{1 - e^{-n k_a \tau}}{1 - e^{-k_a \tau}}\right) e^{-k_a t'} \right]$$
    where $t'$ is the time elapsed since the most recent dose ($0 \le t' < \tau$).
  - Multi-dose IV bolus concentration:
    $$C_n(t') = \frac{\text{Dose}}{V_d} \left(\frac{1 - e^{-n k_e \tau}}{1 - e^{-k_e \tau}}\right) e^{-k_e t'}$$
  - Accumulation Factor ($R_{acc}$):
    $$R_{acc} = \frac{1}{1 - e^{-k_e \tau}}$$
  - Steady-State Concentration Extremes:
    $$C_{ss,\max} = \frac{F \cdot \text{Dose}}{V_d} \cdot \frac{1}{1 - e^{-k_e \tau}}, \quad C_{ss,\min} = C_{ss,\max} \cdot e^{-k_e \tau}$$
    $$\bar{C}_{ss} = \frac{F \cdot \text{Dose}}{CL \cdot \tau}$$
  - Elimination rate constant: $k_e = \frac{CL}{V_d}$; Half-life: $t_{1/2} = \frac{0.693}{k_e}$.

## UX & Accessibility Requirements
- **Interactive Controls**:
  - Dose slider (mg) + discrete `+` / `-` steppers.
  - Interval slider $\tau$ (4h, 6h, 8h, 12h, 24h) + steppers.
  - Clearance slider $CL$ (L/h) + steppers.
  - Volume of Distribution $V_d$ (L) + steppers.
  - Route toggle: Oral vs IV Bolus.
- **Therapeutic Window Visualization**:
  - Shaded green target band $[MEC, MTC]$ (Minimum Effective Concentration to Minimum Toxic Concentration).
  - Real-time safety alert badges:
    - `🟢 In Target Range` (Peak $< MTC$ and Trough $> MEC$).
    - `🔴 Toxicity Warning: Peak C_max exceeds MTC` (risk of concentration-dependent adverse events).
    - `🟡 Subtherapeutic Warning: Trough C_min drops below MEC` (risk of therapeutic failure / resistance).
- **Time-Course Plot**:
  - Vector SVG displaying concentration over consecutive doses ($n = 1$ to $n = 10$).
  - Steady-state dashed guide line at $\bar{C}_{ss}$.
  - WCAG 2.2 keyboard navigation on all interactive inputs.
- **Model Illustration Notice**:
  - Exposes 1-compartment analytical superposition formulas and clinical caveats.
